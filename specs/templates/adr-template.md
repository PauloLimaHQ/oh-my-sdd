## Context

The forces at play: what situation demanded a decision, what constraints
applied, what alternatives were live. Write it so a reader five years from
now understands why this wasn't obvious.

## Decision

The decision itself, in active voice. "We will…" / "X is…". Include the
sharp edges: what this rules out, not just what it enables.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what future work this commits us
to. Honest costs belong here — an ADR that lists only upsides wasn't needed.
